# Irrigation soil bands

Probe scale is 0–100% (higher = wetter).

| Plant group | Target | Water when | Typical session when dry |
|-------------|--------|------------|---------------------------|
| Tomato | 45–62% | driest <45% | 15–25 min soaker |
| Peppers/kale/herbs | 40–65% | driest <40% | 10–18 min soaker |
| Zucchini/eggplant/peas | driest 48–59% OK if wettest <60% | driest <48% (esp. <30%) | **15–25 min** micro — never 1–6 min |
| Corn | 45–62% | driest <45% or no probe + deficit | 12–20 min soaker |
| Hosta/mint/flowers | 40–68% | driest <40% | 10–18 min drip |
| Tall fescue lawn | n/a (often no probe) | cadence / rain deficit | 12–20 min |

Always REPLY `MINUTES: 0` when wettest probe ≥66% or plant is within its skip band.
