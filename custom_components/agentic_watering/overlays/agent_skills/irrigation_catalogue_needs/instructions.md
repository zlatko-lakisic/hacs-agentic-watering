# Catalogue weekly needs (temperate humid peak ET₀ ≈ 31.5 mm/week)

Formula: weekly_need_mm ≈ plant_factor × 31.5

| Plant | Factor | ≈ mm/week | ≈ in/week |
|-------|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Tomato, zucchini, eggplant, sweet corn, hosta | 0.8 (H) | **25** | ~1.0 |
| Pepper, kale, basil, parsley, mint, pea, tall fescue | 0.5 (M) | **16** | ~0.62 |
| Low-water ornamentals (e.g. coneflower) | 0.2 (LO) | **6** | ~0.25 |

Subtract effective recent rainfall (last 36–72 h) and soil adequacy before converting remainder to minutes via flow × area.
